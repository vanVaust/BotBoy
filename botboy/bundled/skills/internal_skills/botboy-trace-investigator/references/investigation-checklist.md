# Investigation Checklist

Use this order during analysis:

1. Identify the failing or slow command.
2. Confirm the principal and request ID.
3. Confirm whether the run completed, errored, or was denied.
4. Inspect span shape and child operations.
5. Check whether cache, approval, auth, or gateway behavior changed the path.
6. Summarize the likeliest cause and the best next validation step.

Do not collapse evidence and diagnosis into one sentence.
State what was observed first, then what it implies.
